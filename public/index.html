How to use CueStarter.com

CueStarter.com helps you break awkward silence with fast conversation starters, icebreaker questions, first date prompts, networking icebreakers, and deeper follow-up ideas. Pick a tone, choose whether you want an exact line or coaching, then tap a prompt that fits the moment.

Say it

Use this mode when you want a ready-to-say icebreaker question for a date, party, chat, coworker, friend, or online match.

Coach me

Use this mode when you want guidance like "ask about their weekend" instead of a scripted line.

Ready next

Keep several conversation prompts available so you can move quickly without losing the flow.

Change topic

Switch to a new topic when the current thread feels done, too serious, or too similar.
